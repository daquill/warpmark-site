Privacy

Short version: Warpmark is in development, this site collects nothing, and your Minecraft sign-in stays on your computer.

This website

This site has no analytics, no cookies and no trackers. It is static pages.

Minecraft sign-in

To play online, the Warpmark launcher asks you to sign in with your Microsoft account. It requests only the permission needed to sign you in to Minecraft (XboxLive.signin, plus offline_access so you stay signed in). The resulting tokens are stored on your own computer in the operating system's credential store. They are never sent to Warpmark's servers, and Warpmark does not read your email, contacts or files.

Warpmark accounts

A Warpmark account, for saving packs and playing with friends, is planned and optional. This page will be updated, and the changes explained, before any account feature is released.

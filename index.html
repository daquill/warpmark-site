Warpmark is a free launcher and mod platform for Minecraft: Java Edition. Search for a mod by name or by what you want it to do, and get a pack that actually installs.

In development. No downloads yet.

What we are building

  • A small, fast launcher for vanilla Minecraft, Fabric, NeoForge, Forge and Quilt, built to stay out of your way.
  • One place to search every mod, with clear notes on what works together.
  • A crash helper that reads your game log and says what went wrong in plain words.
  • Modpacks you can share with friends by link or file, and join each other's games.

Signing in

To play online, Warpmark signs you in with your own Microsoft account, the same account you use for Minecraft. Your Minecraft sign-in is kept on your own computer. It is never sent to Warpmark's servers. See the privacy page.

Free

Warpmark is free to use. We do not sell Minecraft, game files or access to the game, and we do not host Mojang's files: the game is downloaded from Mojang.
